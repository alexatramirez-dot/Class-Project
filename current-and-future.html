Recent Developments

Antitrust ruling (2025)

A federal judge declined to force Google to sell Chrome, but barred exclusive distribution deals and ordered limited data sharing with rivals.

AI browsers (2025 to 2026)

Perplexity Comet and ChatGPT Atlas pushed agentic browsing. Atlas launched in October 2025 and is being shut down in favor of the ChatGPT desktop app.

Incumbents add AI

Chrome added a Gemini side panel and an Auto Browse agent in January 2026.

Future Directions

  • Agentic browsing: assistants that click, fill forms, and finish tasks for the user.
  • Legal and trust questions: Amazon's lawsuit over Comet shows the rules are unsettled.
  • Engine diversity: Ladybird is a from-scratch engine that targets an alpha release in 2026.
  • Regulation: rules on default search and distribution may reshape competition.
