Future Directions

  • AI with user control: Firefox is betting that optional, switch-off-able AI sets it apart from rivals.
  • Engine independence: as Blink dominates, Gecko helps keep web standards from depending on one company.
  • Privacy as a feature: tracking protection remains a main reason to choose Firefox.
  • Funding: reducing dependence on search payments is a long-term challenge.
