History of Mozilla Firefox

On this page:
  • Timeline
  • Lineage
  • Three Phases

Firefox began as a lightweight spin-off of the code Netscape released to the public. Over two decades it went from challenger to Internet Explorer to the last major independent browser.

Timeline

Table 1. Major Firefox milestones
YearMilestone
1998Netscape releases its browser source code, and the Mozilla project begins.
2002Phoenix 0.1 appears as a browser-only alternative to the full Mozilla suite.
2003The Mozilla Foundation is created; Phoenix is renamed Firebird.
2004The browser is renamed Firefox, and Firefox 1.0 launches in November.
2005Mozilla Corporation is formed to develop and distribute Firefox.
2008Firefox 3 sets a record for downloads in a single day.
2011A rapid release cycle begins, starting with Firefox 5.
2017Firefox Quantum (version 57) ships a faster engine with parts built in Rust.
2019Enhanced Tracking Protection turns on by default.
2020Mozilla restructures and cuts about 250 jobs.

Lineage

Three Phases

  • Origins (1998 to 2004): Netscape's code becomes the Mozilla project, and a leaner browser, Firefox, is split out of it.
  • Challenger (2004 to 2010): Firefox pulls users away from Internet Explorer with tabs, add-ons, and better standards support.
  • Rebuild and privacy (2011 to today): faster releases, the Quantum engine work, and a focus on tracking protection as Chrome takes over the market.
