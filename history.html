History of the Web Browser

The browser has changed from a research tool into the most widely used piece of software on most devices. Its history falls into a few phases.

Timeline

Major browser milestones
YearMilestone
1990Tim Berners-Lee builds WorldWideWeb, the first browser and editor, at CERN.
1993NCSA Mosaic brings inline images and a graphical interface to the public.
1994Netscape Navigator launches, and the W3C is founded.
1995Microsoft ships Internet Explorer with Windows, starting the browser wars.
1998Netscape open-sources its code, which becomes the Mozilla project.
2003Apple releases Safari, built on the WebKit engine.
2004Firefox 1.0 revives competition, and the WHATWG forms to evolve HTML.
2007The iPhone brings a desktop-class browser to mobile.
2008Google launches Chrome with the V8 engine and one process per tab.
2014HTML5 becomes a W3C Recommendation.
2020Microsoft rebuilds Edge on Chromium.
2022Internet Explorer is retired.

Three Phases

  • Creation (1990 to 1994): simple tools that proved the idea of hypertext on the internet.
  • Browser wars (1995 to early 2000s): Netscape and Internet Explorer competed, and Internet Explorer came to dominate.
  • Standards and mobile (2004 to today): Firefox, Safari, and Chrome pushed open standards, faster scripting, and mobile browsing.
